Wardrobe Management System: Project Overview


Problem Statement:
Modern individuals often struggle with wardrobe management, leading to decision fatigue, time waste, and unnecessary clothing purchases. 

Key pain points include:

#Lack of Visibility
#Inefficient Outfit Planning
#Redundant Purchases( Buying same outfits again)
#Sustainability & Waste

Scope of the Project:

This project involves designing and developing a digital platform (Web/Mobile App) that acts as a personal virtual stylist and inventory tracker.

In-Scope:

Digital cataloguing of clothing items via photo upload and categorisation (tops, bottoms, shoes, accessories).
Metadata tagging (colour, brand, season, formality, weather suitability).

Out-of-Scope:

Reminder for wardrobe clearance and requirement for newwear 

Target Users:


Busy Professionals
Content Creators
Hostellers or PG people

High-Level Features:


Digital Closet Inventory: Upload, tag, and sort clothing items with custom categories, seasons, colors, and tags.
Virtual Outfit Builder: Mix and match items on a digital canvas to create, save, and favorite complete looks.
Weather & Context Recommendations: Suggest outfits dynamically based on real-time local weather forecasts and the user's upcoming calendar events.

Wardrobe Insights & Statistics: Track metrics such as most/least worn items, cost-per-wear, and overall wardrobe utilization percentage.
